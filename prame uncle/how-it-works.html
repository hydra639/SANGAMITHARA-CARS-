COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about booking our cabs from Salem.

Can I travel anywhere in Tamil Nadu?

Yes! Our database covers all 38 districts of Tamil Nadu including Coimbatore, Madurai, Chennai, Trichy, Ooty, Kanyakumari, Rameswaram, and remote villages. We also serve neighboring corridors like Bangalore, Mysore, and Pondicherry.

How is the road distance calculated?

We calculate driving kilometers via active road network routing algorithms and benchmark corridors across Tamil Nadu for transparent, exact distance measurement.

How quickly will my booking be confirmed?

When you click "Confirm Booking & Send WhatsApp", your ride request is sent directly to our 24/7 dispatch desk. A representative confirms vehicle assignment and driver details within 2 to 5 minutes.

Are round trips and multi-day packages available?

Yes, we offer special multi-day packages for temple tours, hill station family vacations, and corporate travel. Contact our dispatch desk directly at +91 79049 74485 for tailored multi-day itineraries.
