About Our Service

Founded in Salem with a mission to bring complete pricing transparency, reliability, and executive comfort to Tamil Nadu outstation travel.

OUR MISSION

Travel Made Simple & Honest

Based in the central transportation hub of Salem, Tamil Nadu, Mithara Travel Agency was established to eliminate the frustrations commonly associated with commercial outstation cab bookings: arbitrary price surges, hidden driver allowances, and confusing tariff structures.

We introduced a crystal-clear fixed tariff of ₹80 per kilometer. Whether you are driving up the 20 hairpin bends of Yercaud, visiting temples in Madurai or Rameswaram, or traveling for business to Coimbatore, Bangalore, or Chennai, you can calculate your exact road distance and fare in seconds before booking.

✓ Transparent distance calculation via live road routing & GPS navigation
✓ Flat ₹80/km rate across all vehicles with zero surge pricing
✓ 100% verified, courteous local route chauffeurs
✓ Complete coverage across all 38 districts of Tamil Nadu
